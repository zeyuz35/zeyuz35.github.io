# About

Post-doctorate Research Fellow at the Melbourne Business School, University of Melbourne. 
I am currently working with Tomohiro Ando, Mathhew Greenwood-Nimmo, Renee Fry-McKibbin, and Yongcheol Shin. 

Before my current position, I obtained my PhD in Econometrics at Monash University, Department of Econometrics and Business Statistics under the supervision of Bonsoo Koo and Benjamin Wong.
Before that, I received my Bachelor of Commerce (Honours) at Monash University in 2019 and Bachelor of Actuarial Science at Monash University in 2018. 

My research interest lies in high dimensional factor models, structural breaks, and econometric theory for macroeconomics and finance.
